Follows the current part mark. Typing different text unlinks it. Maximum 40 characters. Preview lettering is scaled to fit the box; the actual machine font may differ. Check the reserved width and machine font.

              

              

              
SI face
The flat face that carries the text. The letters match the preview: V front, H rear, O top and U under. Only supported marking faces are offered for this part.
Use the matching face-view button and the DSTV face origins table to check where the text will go.

              
SI X — along the part
The text insertion point measured in millimetres from DSTV X=0 along the positive X direction. For example, X=35 places it 35 mm along the part.
The preview shows X=0 and X+. For a mitre, X=0 is the nearest finished point. Moving the camera does not change these coordinates.

              
SI Q — across the face
Q (written q in the NC1 coordinate guides) is the text insertion point across the selected face, measured in millimetres from that face's q=0 edge in the positive q direction.
The edge and direction depend on the face and profile. Check the DSTV face origins and positive directions table. X and Q locate the text; the angle rotates the reserved text box from that point.

GEOMETRY ACCOUNTING
Every cut must be explained.
Square-cut RHS/SHS, symmetric parallel-flange I-sections (UB/UC), channels and equal/unequal angles. Section radii are preserved and round web/flange/leg holes are verified. The current solid is checked again when you export.
SUPPORTED PROFILES
RHS · SHS · UB/UC · PFC · EA · UA. Axis-aligned members, single solids or STEP assemblies of up to 16 occurrences, up to 16 holes and 16 separate rounded angle edge slots. Simple and compound planar I-section mitres up to 65° total 3D tilt from square, verified spline-encoded I roots and small square end remnants are supported within the selected comparison tolerance (default 0.02 mm). Plates: one constant-thickness solid, square sides, line/arc outline, round holes and internal through-contours; 400 mm diagonal maximum (kernel projection precision). STEP detects supported section or plate geometry and suggests axes; review the origin and directions before export. Ambiguous classification is refused. DXF supports LINE/ARC/CIRCLE/closed LWPOLYLINE with signed bulges and explicit units/layer/thickness. Split broad faces, bevels, countersinks, blind pockets and nested islands are refused. ProCAM plate acceptance is outstanding. I end copes: full flange width, square ends, sharp or tangential circular reliefs, with an intact middle section and retained web. Partial-width cuts, shallow flange-only cuts, drill-style corner reliefs and combined mitre/copes are refused. Profiled RHS/SHS ends: a measured stock frame and four H/U/V/O cutting envelopes include rounded corners within the 0.25 mm ProCAM profile allowance. End-wall bevel angles are not encoded; strict output refuses these non-V cuts. Hidden pockets, separate bores and unbounded/nonplanar end curves remain refused. Multi-plane I ends, combined mitre/copes, tapered/asymmetric I-sections, CHS, unsupported assembly members, unsupported copes, cuts outside the bounded flat-wall routes, blind bores and unrecognised surfaces block export.
