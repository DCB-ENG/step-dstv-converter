GEOMETRY ACCOUNTING

Every cut must be explained.

Square-cut RHS/SHS, symmetric parallel-flange I-sections (UB/UC), channels and equal/unequal angles. Section radii are preserved and round web/flange/leg holes are verified. The current solid is checked again when you export.

SUPPORTED PROFILES

RHS · SHS · UB/UC · PFC · EA · UA. Axis-aligned members, one solid per file, up to 16 holes and 16 separate rounded angle edge slots. Simple I-section mitres up to 65° from square, verified spline-encoded I roots and small square end remnants are supported within the selected comparison tolerance (default 0.02 mm). Plates: one constant-thickness solid, square sides, line/arc outline, round holes and internal through-contours; 400 mm diagonal maximum (kernel projection precision). STEP requires explicit in-plane axes. DXF supports LINE/ARC/CIRCLE/closed LWPOLYLINE with signed bulges and explicit units/layer/thickness. Split broad faces, bevels, countersinks, blind pockets and nested islands are refused. ProCAM plate acceptance is outstanding. I end copes: full flange width, square ends, sharp or tangential circular reliefs, with an intact middle section and retained web. Partial-width cuts, shallow flange-only cuts, drill-style corner reliefs and combined mitre/copes are refused. Profiled RHS/SHS ends: a measured stock frame and four H/U/V/O cutting envelopes include rounded corners within the 0.25 mm ProCAM profile allowance. End-wall bevel angles are not encoded; strict output refuses these non-V cuts. Hidden pockets, separate bores and unbounded/nonplanar end curves remain refused. Compound I mitres, tapered/asymmetric I-sections, CHS, assemblies, unsupported copes, section enclosed slots, blind bores and unrecognised surfaces block export.
